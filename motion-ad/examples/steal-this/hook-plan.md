## Storyboard (0.25 s steps), HOOK_END = 4.93 (track BASS IN at 13.18 s; ad 0 = track 8.25)
- 0.00 frame 0 is the cover: the feed already mid-flick — 3 flat ad cards whipping upward, a big cartoon thumb (solid, lit) on the right, "IF PEOPLE" landed huge.
- 0.25–0.9 IF PEOPLE (letters drop + squash) · the thumb flicks again (anticipation, swipe, overshoot).
- 0.9–1.8 SCROLL: the word rockets up through frame with the cards (motion blur at peak), "RIGHT PAST" rides on a card.
- 1.8–2.6 YOUR ADS: the card it's on is a flat, boring ad ("SALE 20% OFF" placeholder look) that grays out as it passes.
- 2.6–3.0 the dots "…": the feed decelerates.
- 3.0–3.4 STEAL THIS slams down full-width (Archivo wdth/wght punch), the thumb smacks into it: squash, impact ring, the feed STOPS.
- 3.4–4.9 hold: THIS punches, light sweep once; the thumb wiggles stuck; layers drift; at 4.93 the bass lands → iris to scene 2.
