# "Worse song": THE bar for a whole motion ad (made for LYRC)

Verdict on v6: **"thats fucking gorgeous. save it. thats the ideal motion graphic video we want to strive for."**
Watch it (on the kit's page / `example-videos/worse-song.mp4`), read this, then build something for YOUR business that
looks nothing like it.

Files: `sheet.jpg` (one frame per second), `index.html.txt` (the full composition), `narration.json` (the script),
`events.json` + `mix.sh.txt` (voice and sound placements, the mix), `decisions.md` (every ruling made along the way:
an example of how a decisions file grows; its LYRC specifics don't apply to you).

## Why it works
1. **A story hook that acts out its line.** "If you've watched a worse song than yours blow up, here's what they did
   that you didn't." Every spoken word is kinetic type doing what it says: cartoon eyes on WATCHED, a stick band whose
   heads spell W-O-R-S-E, a stadium pull-back on BLOW UP, your stick guy's jaw dropping, your empty slot on DIDN'T.
2. **The body pays the hook off.** "They posted it. Twenty times a day." is ACTED OUT (posts firing around a 24-hour
   clock while a counter climbs to 20), not a number on a still. Then the how, in four real-product steps.
3. **Real everything.** Real UI rebuilt from source, real data, real renders, real clips. Every number checked that day.
4. **Craft.** One continuous camera, every move eased, nothing pulses to the beat, words in the centre of the action,
   clean story moments, real sounds (one per story moment), on-screen audio heard in sync.
