# Story hooks: THE bar for hooks (made for LYRC)

Three takes on one line, "If you want strangers in your comments asking 'who is this?', steal this.", each 5.84 s, each
ACTING OUT the story the line tells. Verdict: **"wtf these are insanely better… that shit is fire"**, then the rule:
*"hooks need to be the most engaging part of the video to get ppl to watch the rest of the video, otherwise they will
scroll and the rest of the video doesn't matter."*
- `A-stick-figures/`: he posts, a cricket hops by, a stick crowd stampedes in with phones, comments spray, a giant hand
  steals his phone on the track's dropout. (Its invented 0→3,481 count would have to go before shipping: no fake numbers.)
- `B-footage-crowd/`: the owner cut out of real footage in a spotlight, a halftone crowd fires comments at him, the whole
  scene turns out to be on a stranger's phone.
- `C-words-act-it-out/` (the pick): every spoken word is kinetic type that does what it says: letter-people spell
  STRANGERS, comment bubbles pile on, WHO IS THIS? gets spray-painted, a heist glove snatches STEAL THIS on the dropout.
What they share: characters + action + a twist, fast (2–9 % calm is fine for a hook), every word readable, real
footage or real product where it fits, the track's dropout or slam doing the work. Learn how; write a new story for each line.
