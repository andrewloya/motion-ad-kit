# Examples: what "good" looks like

Most were made with this kit's rules for LYRC (a lyric-video app for musicians), by its founder loya and Claude,
over ~30 rounds of notes. `steal-this/` was made with the kit itself, for a different business. They are the bar for CRAFT. Never copy their subject, track, layouts, scene order or
hooks: learn how they work, then build something for your business that looks nothing like them.

Each folder has a contact sheet (`sheet.jpg` / `hook-sheet.jpg`, one frame per 0.25–1 s), the full HyperFrames
composition (`index.html.txt`, renamed so it never lints as a second root), and notes. The videos themselves are on
the kit's GitHub page (https://github.com/andrewloya/motion-ad-kit/tree/main/example-videos, or `example-videos/` next to this folder if you downloaded the repo): watch them before your first ad.

| Folder | What it is | Learn |
|---|---|---|
| `worse-song/` | **THE bar for a whole ad.** 34.6 s, narrated. Verdict: "thats fucking gorgeous… the ideal motion graphic video we want to strive for" | a story hook that acts out its line; a body that pays it off with the real product, step by step; real numbers; one camera |
| `claude-connector/` | A whole ad, music-locked, 32.4 s. "i love it. this is fire" (after 20 versions) | the product working inside another app; every cut on the beat; readable; `hook-plan.md` = the 6-hook process done right |
| `story-hooks/` A, B, C | **THE bar for hooks.** Three takes on one line, 5.8 s each. "wtf these are insanely better… that shit is fire" | act out the story the line tells: characters, action, a twist, every word as kinetic type doing what it says. C was the pick |
| `steal-this/` | **Made with THIS kit, for a different business** (the kit itself): a 26 s narrated ad in its own palette, with the full paper trail | the whole build order on a non-LYRC subject: brand.json, 6-hook plan, claims, decisions, critic report, fix rounds |
| `the-lift/` | A hook-only test. "A is fucking SICK" | camera with weight; the track's dropout doing the stealing. One correction: its pushes on the hi-hats are OUT (nothing pulses to the beat) |
